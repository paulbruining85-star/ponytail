## Deliberately not used

- **Culpability decision trees** (e.g. Reason, 1997) — copyrighted, and they
  output a judgment about a person, which this skill does not make.
- **Generic human error probabilities** (e.g. "1 in 1,000" for rule-based
  errors) — context-bound, and the knowledge-based figure in DOE-HDBK-1028-2009
  is sourced to a personal communication. Not suitable for sizing a specific risk.
- **Proprietary root-cause taxonomies** whose owners and licences could not be confirmed.
